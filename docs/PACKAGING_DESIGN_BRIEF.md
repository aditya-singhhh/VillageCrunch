# Thekua packaging: design brief

How the VillageCrunch thekua pouches were designed, and the research behind each choice.

## Principles and how we applied them

| Principle (from research) | Applied as |
|---|---|
| One dominant brand colour block, a small accent per variant. Colour is the first thing a shopper reads. | All three pouches share one warm dawn-sky base. Only the bottom band, badge and name plaque change: wine red (Premium), amber (Jaggery), green (Classic). |
| Warm colours (orange, red, gold) suit indulgent, festive food and raise appetite. Muted, softer tones read as premium. | Peach/gold dawn palette, no neon or heavy saturation. An earlier dark version was dropped as too intense. |
| Gold and deep neutrals signal premium. | Thin gold frame, sun rays and ribbon; maroon (not black) type on cream for strong contrast. |
| Brown/earthy tones read as traditional and natural. | Wooden table, cane and basket tones around the product. |
| Heritage motifs on a modern, simple structure (e.g. Lata Rani, Shuddh Swad). | Chhath Puja sunrise, diyas, sugarcane and a folk border, on a clean layout. |
| Simple front-of-pack hierarchy: brand, product, appetising photo, one claim, weight. | Ribbon brand, big "Thekua", photo medallion, one round claim badge, name plaque, net weight. |
| Only claims you can support. | Claims still need confirming (see below). |
| Check mockups against true-colour print proofs. | Print PDFs are RGB. Ask the printer for a proof. |

## Still to confirm before printing
- Nutrition tables: Premium and Jaggery lack carbohydrate and total sugars. Jaggery's "Added Sugar 63 g" looks inconsistent.
- Ingredient lists differ between the labels you supplied.
- Claims: "100% Shudh Desi Ghee", "Made with Jaggery, No Refined Sugar", "Traditional Bihari Recipe".
- The official FSSAI logo is not drawn; add the registered artwork.
- Pouch die-line size, MFD/batch area and a real product photoshoot.
This is not legal advice. Ask your FSSAI advisor.
